Methodology Spotlight: Handling False Positives (Objective 12)

False Positive Case 1: Festival Streaming Spikes (Onam / Vishu) — A residential customer streams high-definition video during family gatherings, generating 250 GB of usage and triggering a midnight spike alert. Remediation: Incorporate regional holiday calendar covariates and dampening multipliers during state holidays.

False Positive Case 2: Multi-Member Split Recharges — Multiple family members independently recharge the same fibre account within an hour, tripping the recharge velocity alert. Remediation: Cross-reference payment instruments (UPI VPA / Card hash); if distinct payer IDs are verified with successful 2FA, suppress the fraud alert.
